Zigo Partner Par Bookings Kam Kyun Hain? Janiye Asli Wajah Aur Bada Fayda.

Zigo parivar mein aapka swagat hai! Hum jante hain ki kaafi partners ye soch rahe hain ki abhi ride requests thodi kam kyun aa rahi hain. Hum aapke sath 100% transparency rakhna chahte hain.

Sabse Bada Fayda Aapka Hai!

Market ki dusri badi companiyan aapki mehnat ki kamai ka 30% se 40% hissa commission ke naam par kaat leti hain. Lekin Zigo specifically drivers ke fayde ke liye banaya gaya hai.

Hum aapse 0% Commission lete hain! Aapko sirf ek baar ₹49 ID Activation Fee deni hoti hai. Customer se milne wala 100% paisa direct aapki jeb mein jayega.

Thoda sabar rakhein aur apni duty ko ON rakhein. Jo partners is shuruwati daur mein Zigo ke sath jude rahenge, unhe aage chal kar sabse zyada fayda hone wala hai. 100% kamai aapki, kyunki Zigo driver ka apna app hai!
