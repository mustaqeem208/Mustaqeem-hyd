About ZIGO Partner

ZIGO Partner – Driver App & Ride Coordination

ZIGO Partner is a lightweight, zero-commission driver app designed to help drivers easily connect with nearby customers for seamless ride coordination.

The app is built to provide a simple, efficient, and transparent experience. Receive nearby ride requests, view exact pickup and drop-off locations, and manage your daily trips smoothly—all while keeping 100% of your earnings.

Key Features:

  • Easy Access: Quick and secure login using your mobile number.
  • Nearby Rides: Instantly receive ride requests from customers around you.
  • Clear Navigation: View pickup and drop-off locations clearly before accepting a ride.
  • Flexible Work: Toggle your status Online or Offline anytime—be your own boss.
  • Simple UI: A clean, easy-to-use interface designed specifically for drivers.
  • Optimised Performance: Built to consume very low mobile data and battery power.

ZIGO Partner is the perfect solution for drivers seeking a straightforward, minimal app to coordinate rides without heavy commission cuts.
