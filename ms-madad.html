About MS Madad Foundation

MS Madad Foundation is dedicated to uplifting underserved communities through timely humanitarian aid, healthcare support, and educational empowerment. We believe lasting change begins when communities stand together for those in need.

Our Core Welfare Initiatives

  • Food & Ration Drives: Distributing monthly grocery and essential ration kits to daily-wage and low-income households.
  • Medical & Emergency Relief: Assisting patients with urgent treatments, life-saving medicines, and hospital support.
  • Education & Youth Empowerment: Providing books, school fees, and learning resources so no child drops out due to financial hardship.
  • Community & Disaster Help: Rapid on-ground relief during floods, extreme weather, and local crises.
