YouTube RPM Kaise Increase Karein: Complete Practical Guide

Agar aapke channel par views to aa rahe hain lekin earnings kam ho rahi hain, to problem views me nahi balke RPM (Revenue Per Mille) me hai. Niche di gayi verified strategies se aap apna RPM double kar sakte hain.

Advertisement

1. High-Paying Advertiser Niches Target Karein

Har category me barabar ad rate nahi milta. Finance, tech software, application tutorials aur business categories me advertiser sabse zyada bid lagate hain.

  • High RPM Categories: App reviews, technical tutorials, business setup guides aur digital earning ideas.
  • Optimization: Video title aur tags me commercial intent keywords shamil karein.

2. 8-Minute Se Lambi Videos & Mid-Roll Setup

8 minute se badi video me aap manual Mid-Roll Ads add kar sakte hain. Automatic breaks ke bajaye 2 se 3 natural breaks manually set karein taaki user experience kharab na ho aur revenue boost ho sake.

Advertisement

3. Tier-1 Countries Audience Targeting

Agar aapki video me English subtitles (CC) hain ya video aisi hai jise international users samajh sakein, to RPM direct 3x se 5x ho jata hai.

Action Tip: English CC captions add karein aur description me keywords English me bhi mention karein.

4. High Retention Format & Thumbnail Hook

Advertiser unhi videos par sabse mehenge ads place karta hai jinki Average View Duration (AVD) 50% se upar ho.

  • Pehle 15-20 seconds me video ka direct conclusion na dein, balke exact roadmap samjhayein.
  • Chapters aur timestamps zaroor add karein taaki user easily navigate kare aur bounce rate kam rahe.
