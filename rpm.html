YouTube RPM Kaise Increase Karein: Complete Practical Guide

Agar aapke channel par views to laakhon me hain lekin earnings us hisaab se nahi ban rahi, to masla views ka nahi balke RPM (Revenue Per Mille) ka hai. Jaanie wo actionable settings aur content strategies jisse har 1,000 views par aapka revenue 2x se 3x boost hoga.

Advertisement

1. High-Paying Advertiser Niches Target Karein

Sabhi topics par advertisers barabar bid nahi lagate. Comedy, vlogging ya shorts ke muqable technical aur finance-related videos par high CPM bidding aati hai.

  • Premium RPM Topics: Online Earning Tools, Software/App Reviews, Business ideas, Tech Guides, Coding aur Logistics solutions.
  • Action Step: Title aur Description me advertiser-friendly phrases dalein jaise "How to configure", "Setup guide", "Best alternatives".

2. Video Length 8 Minute Se Zyada Aur Strategic Mid-Rolls

8 minute se lambi videos par YouTube creators ko manually Mid-Roll Ads lagane ki sahulat deta hai. Lekin automatic placements par rely karne ke bajaye manual break points set karein.

Pro Placement Tip: Video ke boring parts par ad mat lagayein. Jahan aap koi suspension ya important step complete karne ja rahe hon (natural cliffhanger), wahan mid-roll lagayein taaki viewer ad skip na kare.
Advertisement

3. Tier-1 Countries Se Watch-Time Attract Karein

Agar aapki Hindi ya bilingual video par US, UK, UAE ya Canada se 10% views bhi aate hain, to pura channel RPM jump kar jata hai.

  1. English Subtitles (CC): Har video me clear English subtitles upload karein. YouTube algorithm bahar ke users ko bhi recommendations me bhejna shuru kar deta hai.
  2. Bilingual Metadata: Description aur chapters me English technical terms zaroor include karein.

4. High Retention Format & Thumbnail Hook

Advertiser unhi videos par sabse mehenge ads place karta hai jinki Average View Duration (AVD) 50% se upar ho.

  • Pehle 15-20 seconds me video ka direct conclusion na dein, balke suspense aur exact roadmap samjhayein.
  • Chapters aur timestamps zaroor add karein taaki user easily navigate kare aur bounce rate kam rahe.
